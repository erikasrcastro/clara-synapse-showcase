# Business value

Clara is designed around a simple question:

**Which opportunity needs attention before it goes cold?**

The pilot focuses on:
- reducing forgotten follow-ups;
- making open commercial value visible;
- shortening the time needed to prepare a follow-up;
- keeping the operator in control before messages are sent;
- fitting the routine of a small business without becoming a complex CRM.

## Target audience

Service providers and MEIs who receive quote requests and commercial conversations through WhatsApp.
