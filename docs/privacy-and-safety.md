# Privacy and safety approach

The Clara pilot is designed with:
- passwordless sign-in plus mandatory MFA;
- per-user data isolation;
- minimum-data access for AI generation;
- server-side secret handling;
- explicit WhatsApp contact consent;
- opt-out enforcement;
- no autonomous AI sending;
- a hard pilot budget ceiling for paid WhatsApp messaging.

This showcase contains synthetic/public information only. It does not contain customer data, source code, credentials, internal prompts or security-sensitive configuration.
